# interpret: the writing side

## Goal

`@orkestrel/interpret` is the reason family's one owner of wording. From a `reason` definition and its result it writes a true, worded account with its evidence; it ships every general word, each replaceable on its own; and a consumer supplies the domain's words, whose absence an authoring check reports.

## Exit criterion

1. `Narrator` writes three documents from `reason`'s own types: a verdict with its reasons, the worksheet of one number, and the guide to a definition's rules.
2. Every truth rule in `designs/writing.md` § Truth holds under its named proof, each with a mutation probe on its deciding line.
3. A missing domain word is reported by `audit`, and at run time prints the field's path or the part's id and travels as data, never blank and never a throw.
4. One word of the general lexicon can be replaced without restating its table.
5. The package's gates are green, and an independent review passes on numbered claims.

## Governing skills

`orkestrel-scout` before a brief, `orkestrel-harden` (a capability selection) for the package units, `orkestrel-dispatch` and `orkestrel-parallel` for the lanes, `orkestrel-falsify` for a repair round, and `orkestrel-publish` for the release.

## Claims and release bar

The claims are the truth rules and the exit criterion. The bar for this campaign: no claim broken, every gate green, and no finding of the review left open that a reader of the account could meet. Wording taste is not a finding.
